Facebook brought you 4 enquiries for $365 — about $91 each. Google is more expensive per enquiry. It buys a different person.

On Google, they typed "bathroom remodel near me" that morning. Nobody types that by accident. They are further along, and they are comparing two or three contractors right now — which is why being at the top of that moment matters.

What we would do with it

  • Your towns only. Never the whole state. This is the single biggest way money gets wasted, and it is the default setting.
  • Only people searching right now — not people "interested in" your area, which is how you end up paying for someone in Paramus.
  • Your phone number inside the ad, so they can call without even reaching the website.
  • Your booking link, as soon as your calendar is set up, so the person looking at ten at night can grab a time instead of disappearing.
  • Start small — around $15 a day for two weeks, then look at what actually came in before spending another dollar.
