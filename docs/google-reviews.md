slots well, and build a 2027 pipeline from better-paying towns.

That makes qualification the whole ballgame, and it retires any "book a free
estimate today" framing.

## Advertising — Geza's answer to ask #8

- **Facebook only to start.** No budget for multiple platforms yet.
- **$300–400, up to $500, and not necessarily every month.**
- The Modern Master Facebook page currently runs **under Sam's personal
  account**. Geza asks whether to open a new one under his own name.

### On the Facebook account question

Recommendation: **do not create a new page. Move the existing one.**

A Facebook Page cannot be owned by a business on its own — a personal profile
always sits behind it. The fix is not a new page, it is a **Meta Business
Portfolio in Geza's name** that the existing page is transferred into. That
keeps the page's followers, history and age, all of which a brand-new page
loses and all of which affect how ads perform.

The real argument is continuity, not convenience: as things stand, if Sam
became unreachable, Geza would lose the page and everything on it. That is a
business asset sitting in someone else's account.

Sequence:
1. Geza creates a Business Portfolio at business.facebook.com under his own
   profile.
2. Sam adds Geza as full admin of the page, then transfers the page into
   Geza's portfolio.
3. Geza creates the ad account inside his own portfolio, with his own card.
4. Marcelo gets partner access — not ownership.

### Before a single dollar is spent

The site has **no GA4 tag and no Meta pixel**. `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID` is wired
but unset in production, and nothing fires on form submit. Spending $400 a
month with no conversion signal means Meta cannot optimise, we cannot
retarget, and we cannot tell a good campaign from a bad one. This is the one
thing that has to be done first.

Also: both social icons in `src/components/Footer.tsx` are `href="#"` — dead
on the live site. Fix once we have the real page URL, and add it to `sameAs`
in the JSON-LD, which is currently missing.
