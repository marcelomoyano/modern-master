How to Shoot the Video

Your phone. About 30 seconds. No editing, no music, no filters.

Before you hit record

  • Hold the phone vertical — up and down, not sideways.
  • Turn on every light. Open the blinds.
  • Wipe the mirror and the glass.
  • Clear the room — no tools, bags, or towels on the floor.
  • Wear the Modern Master polo.
  • Close the door behind you so it's quiet.

Where to stand

  • Start in the doorway. Walk in slowly while you talk, and stop when the whole bathroom is in frame.
  • Phone at chest height, both hands. Steady beats fancy.

What to say — five things, in this order

  1. Who you are. "I'm Geza. I own Modern Master."
  2. Where you're standing. "This is a full bathroom I just finished in Hillsborough."
  3. One thing you're proud of — point at it. "I built this shower pan on site, and I set every one of these tiles by hand."
  4. How long it took. "Start to finish, about three weeks. I was here every day — no crew you've never met."
  5. What you want them to do. "If you want yours done right, give me a call."

The rules

  • Talk like a neighbor, not an ad. If it sounds like a commercial, people scroll past.
  • Don't script it. Know the five things, then say them your own way.
  • Mess up? Start over. It costs nothing. Nobody gets it first try.
  • Shoot it three times and send all three. Don't pick a favorite.
  • No music, no filters, no captions. We add anything it needs on our end.
  • Text or AirDrop the original to Marcelo. Don't post it anywhere first — uploading to Instagram or Facebook squashes the quality and we can't get it back.
Don't say a price in the video yet. Once we've settled exactly what's included and what materials run on top, we'll ask you to shoot one more line about cost. Until then, leave numbers out — a price on video is hard to walk back.
